About DOVE

A family crew, building Cushing one home at a time

Don Huckabay has worked in construction since 1981 — roofing crews, new-home builders, apartment rehab teams. In 2012, he and Verna started flipping houses together. In 2015, they founded DOVE Construction.

Verna was a school teacher before she co-founded the company, and she brings the design eye — color coordination, layout, organization. Their son Alan helps run the company day to day. The crew is six people with 75+ years of combined experience:
